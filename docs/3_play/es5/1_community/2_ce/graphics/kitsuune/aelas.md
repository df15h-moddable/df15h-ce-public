# AELAS

Avanced Exterior Lighting And Skies. Successor to EVLaS.

- "improves Skyrims exterior lighting by dynamically synchronizing it with its source"
- "realistic orbital simulation"
- "seasonal daylength variation"
- "direct and volumetric lighting that makes sense"
- "dynamic ambient lighting ... an improved version of the KreateE DALC fix"
- "well-documented, powerful configuration"

Dependencies ...

- is a KiLoader plugin

It furthermore reworks all sky objects to have realistic orbits and adds variable seasonal day lengths.
