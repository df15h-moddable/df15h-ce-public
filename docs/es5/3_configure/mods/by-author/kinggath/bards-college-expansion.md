# Bards College Expansion

## Patches

Patches that reconcile myriad mods with "Bards College Expansion" are squirreled away at `forum.kinggathcreations`. Why ...?

- It seems, per terms, Bethesda doesn't want to assume responsiblity, even indirectly.
- Nexus Mods, per terms, doesn't allow mods that depend upon paid mods.
- Therefore, patches can neither be hosted at `bethesda.net` nor `nexusmods.com`.

The unofficial patch collection can be found here ...

- [Bards College Expansion - Unofficial Patches](https://forum.kinggathcreations.com/resources/bards-college-expansion-unofficial-patches.5/) by cavy8 @ forum.kinggathcreations.
